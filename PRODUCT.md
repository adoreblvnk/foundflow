# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Frontline found-property staff at airports, transport operators, hotels and other venues. They work through a custody intake while handling a physical item and need to record nested contents quickly without losing evidence or accountability.

## Product Purpose

FoundFlow turns guided photographs and staff observations into a structured, evidence-linked inventory draft. Success means staff can capture a complex container, resolve uncertain details, approve the manifest and export it to an existing records system without surrendering the final decision to AI.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise and trustworthy. The interface should feel like an operational tool that helps staff complete a custody record, not an autonomous AI showcase.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic chatbot UI, decorative AI gradients, consumer marketplace styling, dense command-centre theatrics and interfaces that expose internal model mechanics instead of the intake task.

## Design Principles

- Keep the physical evidence and nested inventory relationship visible.
- Make uncertainty actionable and human approval explicit.
- Preserve familiar form, review and export affordances.
- Show provenance through evidence links and audit history.
- Record notes and coins as separate currency-and-denomination groups; compute exact totals from denomination × quantity and never guess unreadable values.
- Keep deterministic demo fixtures clearly separate from live AI results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support and text alternatives for evidence images. Do not rely on colour alone for status.
