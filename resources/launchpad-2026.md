# Launchpad 2026 AI Challenge

## Deliverables

- Repository, public or with judge access
- Demo video, maximum 3 minutes
- Write-up, maximum 1,000 words, using the five judging pillars as headings
- Participant profile, short judge message and resume

## Judging pillars

1. Problem
2. Approach
3. Evidence
4. Constraints
5. Honesty & Trajectory

## Dates

- Challenge began: 3 July 2026
- Submission deadline: 31 July 2026, 23:59 SGT
- Finalists announced: 7 August 2026
- Symposium: 17–18 August 2026 at NUS

## Project implication

A modest claim backed by a reproducible operational comparison is stronger than a broad lost-and-found platform claim. The submission should prove the intake workflow using measured completion time, omission rate, item recall, correction burden, latency and cost.
